I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Syracuse University, graduating in **July 2026**. I work with [Prof. Bryan Kim](https://sites.google.com/view/bryansjkim/home) at the [Syracuse Sustainable Storage Systems Lab (S4 Lab)](https://thecreativecoa.wixsite.com/s4lab). My research sits at the intersection of **storage systems and machine learning infrastructure** — I build systems that make large-scale ML training pipelines faster, more cost-efficient, and more resilient at the I/O and storage layer.

My recent work includes **Seneca** ([FAST '26](https://www.usenix.org/conference/fast26/presentation/desai)), a system that decouples and overlaps data preprocessing with ML training to eliminate I/O bottlenecks and improve GPU utilization, and **Cicero** ([EuroMLSys '26](https://euromlsys.eu/)), which enables cost-efficient large model training and checkpointing on preemptible cloud VMs. I hold **two US patents** from research internships at Samsung Semiconductor, where I worked on key-value SSD storage architectures.

Before my PhD, I was a **Software & Data Engineer at [Practo](https://practo.com)** (India's largest telemedicine platform), where I owned the production-scale user behavior and events ingestion pipeline that powered data analytics, ML, and recommendations infrastructure. I have deep experience with distributed systems, key-value stores (LevelDB, RocksDB), and large-scale data pipelines.

I am actively seeking **Research Scientist, Research Engineer, and Systems Engineer** positions starting **July 2026**. Feel free to reach out at [omkarbdesai@gmail.com](mailto:omkarbdesai@gmail.com).
