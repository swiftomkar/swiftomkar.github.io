Hello! I am Omkar Desai, PhD Student and Research Assistant at the Syracuse Sustainable Storage Systems Lab (S4 Lab). I am a part of the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Syracuse University. Before joining graduate school for my PhD, I was a Software and Data Engineer at [Practo Technologies](https://practo.com) which is the biggest telemedicine platform in India. I have also worked on IoT in Behavioural Science research at [Delos Living] (https://delos.com).
I am interested in computer systems with a particular focus on data storage systems.
